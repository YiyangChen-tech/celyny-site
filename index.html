Connection

Pick the XDS110 Class Application/User UART port. The baud rate must match DPT_BAUD in dpt_params.m (230400).

Double pulse

Connect to the board, set the timing, then Apply. The board always starts with the outputs off.

Sine PWM

Complementary outputs ePWM3A (GPIO4) and ePWM3B (GPIO5) with 0.5 µs dead time at every frequency. The frequency can be changed while running; it switches at a carrier-period boundary. Enable also sends the frequency in the box. Modulation index and fundamental are SINE_M and SINE_F0 in dpt_params.m. STOP also turns the sine PWM off. The board always starts with it off at 20 kHz.
